The country data in the resources/ directory (countries.tsv), and its
compiled-in copy in dasmig/countrygen_data.hpp, is a database built from:

1. mledoze/countries (https://github.com/mledoze/countries)
   Licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0.
   https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1.0/

2. REST Countries (https://gitlab.com/restcountries/restcountries)
   Licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0.
   https://www.mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/

3. World Bank Open Data (https://data.worldbank.org/)
   Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

4. Unicode CLDR (https://cldr.unicode.org/), supplemental territoryInfo.
   Copyright (c) Unicode, Inc. Licensed under the Unicode License v3.
   https://www.unicode.org/license.txt
   Used for the language_shares column: the share of each territory's
   population that speaks each language, and the English language names.

5. SIL ISO 639-3 code tables (https://iso639-3.sil.org/), free to use, for
   the names of languages that CLDR does not name.
   https://iso639-3.sil.org/code_tables/download_tables#terms

- The CLDR language data is under the Unicode License v3, which asks that

Locale tables
-------------

The locale tables (locales.tsv, currencies.tsv, zones.tsv, zone_links.tsv,
holidays.tsv, holiday_events.tsv and holiday_years.tsv in resources/, and
their compiled-in copy in dasmig/countrygen_locale_data.hpp) are built by
scripts/prepare_locales.py, prepare_zones.py and prepare_holidays.py from:

6. Unicode CLDR 47 (cldr-json), for locales.tsv and the cash digits of
   currencies.tsv: date, time, number and currency formats, names, week
   data and measurement systems. Copyright (c) Unicode, Inc. Licensed under
   the Unicode License v3 (https://www.unicode.org/license.txt), whose
   notice must stay with copies of the data.

7. ISO 4217 lists one and three, as published by SIX Group
   (https://www.six-group.com/en/products-services/financial-information/data-standards.html),
   for currencies.tsv: the codes, numeric codes and minor units.

8. The IANA time zone database (https://www.iana.org/time-zones), release
   2026f as compiled in the PyPI tzdata package 2026.6, for zones.tsv and
   zone_links.tsv. In the public domain.

9. GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org/), cities1000, for the population
   share of each zone in zones.tsv and the zones of countries IANA does not
   list. Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
   (CC BY 4.0), which requires credit to GeoNames.

10. python-holidays 0.107 (https://github.com/vacanza/holidays), for the

From countries.tsv above, the locale tables take each country's code and
currency code, and, where the sources above have nothing, its first
language (a fallback locale) and its first UTC offset (a fallback zone).
Each locale table names its sources in its first lines.
